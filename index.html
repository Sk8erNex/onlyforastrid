A little gift from my heart

For Astrid

Some things are too important to fit into a simple message. So I made you this.

Astrid,

I honestly don’t know if I’ll ever find the right words to explain how much you mean to me. I could write a thousand letters and still feel like none of them would be enough.

There’s something about you that I can’t put into words. Maybe it’s your pretty face, those beautiful eyes of yours that I could get lost in forever, or the way you’re almost the same height as me, something I secretly find incredibly cute.

But it’s not just the way you look. It’s you. Every little thing about you has become something I care about more than I probably should.

I really, truly like you, Astrid. And that’s what scares me the most.

Because when you genuinely like someone, the thought of losing them hurts in a way you can’t explain. I don’t want to wake up one day and have you become just a memory. I don’t want to look back at our moments together and wish I had said more, liked you harder, or told you how important you were to me.

You mean so much more to me than I know how to express. Your presence makes things feel different. Better. And somehow, you've become someone I never want to imagine my life without.

If I could ask for one thing, it would simply be for you to stay. Stay when things are difficult. Stay when I’m not perfect. Stay when I don’t know how to put my feelings into words.

I don’t need everything to be perfect. I just want you.

I love your eyes. I love your face. I love being close to you. But more than all of that, I like the person you are, and I hope you understand just how special you are to me.

Astrid, I don’t want to lose you. ❤️

And if there ever comes a moment when you doubt how much I like you, please remember this:

Somewhere in this world, there is someone who looks at you and thinks, “I really hope I get to keep this girl in my life.”

That someone is me.

I like you more than I know how to say.

I want the late night drives with our blend, with my hand on your thigh. Buying flowers from grandmas to support them because you don’t like flowers that die after like 2 days.

I want my dream date with you, Astrid. You are the girl I ever dreamed of, and now I lost that dream girl and it hurts more than everything ever could hurt me…

❤️ I like you, Astrid. ❤️
